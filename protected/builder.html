Saving and privacy

Your resume is saved automatically to your LUXCV account, with a working copy kept in this browser while you edit.

  • Only you can open it while you are signed in, from any browser or device. Use Log out in My Account when you finish on a shared computer.
  • If your connection drops, edits stay in this browser and are sent to your account when you are back online.
  • Don’t put passwords, ID numbers or other secrets in your resume.
  • To keep a copy outside LUXCV, use Save as PDF. A profile photo is saved too, shrunk to about 50 KB.

Clear entire resume?

This removes everything you have entered, including your saved resume in your account. Your chosen template stays selected. This can’t be undone.

Load saved resume?

This replaces what is on the page with the copy saved to your account.

Delete all saved data?

This permanently removes your saved resume from your account and this browser, along with your saved template choice, and clears the editor. It can’t be undone. Resumes you saved as PDF files are not affected.

Save as PDF

LUXCV creates PDFs with your browser’s own print window, so the text stays sharp and selectable. Nothing is downloaded until you save from that window.

  1. Choose Save as PDF as the destination (Chrome, Edge) or PDF → Save as PDF (Safari, Firefox).
  2. Leave margins on Default or None and turn Background graphics on if you see it.
  3. Press Save.
